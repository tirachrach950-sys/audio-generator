Questions, answered

    

      
Which voices can I use?
Voices come from each visitor’s device, so the accents available depend on the browser and system. Microsoft Edge offers the widest choice. If an accent is missing, the studio shows the closest voices instead.

      
Is my script private?
Listening happens in your browser. When you download an MP3, your script is sent to our server to generate the audio, and we do not store it.

      
Do I need a microphone?
No. Your voiceover is generated from text, so you never record anything.
How long can my script be?
Each MP3 can be up to 5,000 characters, roughly five minutes of speech. For longer videos, download your script in parts.

      
Does it work on mobile?
Yes. Mobile browsers require a tap to start audio, which is why the embed uses a button.
